Airdeck

Mix with your bare hand. One phone watches your fingers, the other shows a 3D DJ deck and plays the sound.

Open this page on both phones. They pair with a short code, no account needed.

Starting
Prop this phone up so the camera sees your hand inside the dashed box. Pinch your thumb and finger to press, and hold the pinch to drag.

Connect to the player phone

Type the code shown on the player phone, or scan its QR code to open this page with the code filled in.

Touch mode
Pinch to press. Hold a pinch and move to drag a fader. Touch works too.
